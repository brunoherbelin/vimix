# vimix features

vimix is a real-time video mixer for creating live visuals, VJ sets, and
projection-mapped compositions. It combines video, images, and generated
graphics in a single workspace, with hands-on controls for mixing and shaping
the output.

## Build a mix from different sources

Add video and audio files, still images, image sequences, screen or camera
captures, and network streams. You can also create text, animated patterns,
shader-based graphics, or use a saved session as a source. Media playback can
be controlled from vimix, and supported media sources can provide audio.

## Compose and transform visuals

The workspace provides different ways to work with sources:

- **Mixing:** blend sources by changing their opacity and blend mode.
- **Geometry:** position, scale, rotate, crop, and reshape sources, including
  perspective adjustments for projection mapping.
- **Layers:** organize sources by their front-to-back order.
- **Texturing:** crop or repeat a texture and apply standard or hand-drawn
  masks.
- **Transitions:** move smoothly between sessions.

Group or clone sources to build reusable compositions and apply effects
independently.

## Apply real-time image effects

Add image-processing effects to sources, including color adjustments, blur,
sharpening, keying, and other shader-based filters. The filter chain and
shader editor provide further ways to shape a source's appearance.

## Save, reuse, and perform sessions

Save a composition as a `.mix` session, maintain versions, and organize
sessions in playlists. Control vimix interactively with the mouse, keyboard,
gamepads, or Open Sound Control (OSC). Ableton Link can synchronize musical
tempo and transport with compatible applications.

## Display, stream, and record the output

Send the composition to connected displays or projectors, record it as a video,
or broadcast it over SRT. vimix can also share video with other vimix instances
on a local network or through shared memory.
